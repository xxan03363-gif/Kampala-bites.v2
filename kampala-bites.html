Started on the roadside. Still cooked the same way.

Kampala Bites started small — a single charcoal stove, a stack of chapatis, and a corner in the city where the morning rush needed something hot and fast. What began as one person frying rolexes before sunrise has grown into a full menu, but the way we cook hasn't changed.

Every order is still made fresh when you ask for it. No warming trays, no pre-made batches sitting around. If it's on the menu, it goes on the pan after you order.
